## 3. Per-spec findings (drafts 09–14)

### 09 — Subjects & Teaching Assignments
- **P09-01 (Medium)** Roll-over (07) does not carry `class_subjects` forward; admins re-enter every assignment yearly. Add "copy assignments from previous year" to roll-over.
- **P09-02 (Medium)** Changing `teacher_id` for a substitute rewrites history; changing `kkm` after grades exist reclassifies Tuntas/Belum Tuntas retroactively. Define whether these are locked once grades exist.
- **P09-03 (Low)** Open question on subject grouping (Kelompok A/B/Muatan Lokal) should be resolved before 11, since report card layout depends on it.

### 10 — Assessment & Grading
- **P10-01 (High)** `grade_categories` are really individual assessments (they carry `assessed_on`). Common practice weights by *type* (Tugas 30%, UH 30%, PTS 20%, PAS 20%) and averages instances within a type. Suggest two levels: assessment types (weighted) → assessments.
- **P10-02 (High)** No term scoping (see S-02).
- **P10-03 (Medium)** Undefined: null scores (count as 0 or exclude?) and mixed weighted/unweighted assessments in one course.
- **P10-04 (Medium)** No finalize/lock state, yet 11 depends on "finalized grades submitted".
- **P10-05 (Medium)** Curriculum terminology: Kurikulum Merdeka uses KKTP and per-subject achievement descriptions, not only numeric KKM. Decide which curriculum(s) v1 targets.
- **P10-06 (Low)** Deleting an assessment cascades grades "with audit warning", but no audit store exists (S-06).

### 11 — Digital Report Cards
- **P11-01 (High)** No snapshot on publish. Grades edited after publication change the on-screen report while downloaded PDFs keep the old values. Snapshot the payload (and/or PDF) at publish time.
- **P11-02 (Medium)** Mid-semester class transfer: subject grades from the previous class's `class_subjects` are dropped. Define attribution.
- **P11-03 (Medium)** End-of-year report needs a promotion decision (naik / tidak naik kelas) that should feed roll-over (07) instead of manual retention mapping.
- **P11-04 (Medium)** Official rapor includes extracurriculars, but Ekskul is a v2 candidate. At minimum add a free-text ekskul section.
- **P11-05 (Low)** `principal_notes` is edited in the homeroom teacher workspace; depends on S-01.
- **P11-06 (Low)** `report_periods.is_active` semantics undefined (several active at once?).

### 12 — Announcements
- **P12-01 (Medium)** Attachment disk left as "public or private". Class-scoped attachments must be private with authorized download.
- **P12-02 (Medium)** No read/acknowledge tracking — key for circulars needing parental consent (study tour, permission slips).
- **P12-03 (Low)** Subject teachers cannot post to classes they teach; only homeroom. Audience cannot target a grade level (depends on S-03).

### 13 — Timetable
- **P13-01 (Medium)** Free-form `start_time`/`end_time` per slot. Schools use a fixed bell schedule ("Jam ke-1 07:00–07:35"); add a periods master (with a shorter-Friday variant) and bind slots to periods.
- **P13-02 (High)** `student.class_id` in dashboard query (S-05).
- **P13-03 (Low)** No term scoping (S-02).

### 14 — School Fees (highest risk)
- **P14-01 (High)** No void/correction for recorded payments. Payments should be immutable; corrections recorded as reversal entries with a reason and actor.
- **P14-02 (High)** Cashier may pay multiple bills in one transaction, but `payments.bill_id` is singular. Needs a payment-allocation table.
- **P14-03 (High)** `bills.status = pending_verification` duplicates state derivable from `payments`. AC-14-05 reverts a rejected transfer to `unpaid` even if the bill was already `partially_paid`. Derive bill status from verified/pending payments.
- **P14-04 (Medium)** `waived` is all-or-nothing; no partial discount/scholarship (e.g. 50% SPP, sibling discount).
- **P14-05 (Medium)** Student leaving mid-year: future bills are not cancelled.
- **P14-06 (Medium)** Receipt number generation under concurrent cashiers is unspecified (race on `KWT-YYYYMM-NNNNN`).
- **P14-07 (Low)** `bill_month` "1–12, January… or July…" is ambiguous; pick calendar month + `bill_year`.
- **P14-08 (Medium)** `treasurer` role does not exist (S-01).
- **P14-09 (Low)** School bank account details for transfer instructions are not specified anywhere (S-04).
