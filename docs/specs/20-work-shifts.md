# 20 — Work Shifts (Jadwal Shift Kerja)

Status: draft v1.1 (2026-10-26) — reviewed against the shipped spec 16 implementation; ready for implementation planning. Supersedes draft v1.0 (2026-10-05): shift management settled as a section of the attendance settings page and the roster on the flat `staff-attendance/roster` URL; roster rows may mark an explicit day off (nullable `shift_id`); roster changes working hours only, never expected days; a closing tap or manual override on an existing record uses the record's snapshotted shift, not the effective shift of the tap date; manual override is amended for cross-midnight checkout times; "tidak absen pulang" is counted only after the effective shift end has passed; shift deletion is blocked by attendance snapshots too; the pre-start (−30 min) attribution window was dropped as unnecessary (record dates are calendar-based); AC-20-11 was corrected for the configurable debounce window.

> **Motivation:** Spec 16 assumes a single daily working window for all employees. In reality, non-teaching staff (security / satpam, cleaning / OB, lab assistants) frequently work on rotating shifts with different start/end times and may include evening or night shifts. Without shift support, these employees are incorrectly flagged as late or absent because their actual hours differ from the global `staff_start_time` / `staff_end_time`.

## Problem

1. **False lateness**: A night-shift security guard arriving at 19:00 is flagged as late because `staff_start_time` is 07:00.
2. **False absences**: The morning sweep at `staff_absent_sweep_time` (09:00) marks night-shift employees absent because they have no check-in before 09:00 — they are not expected until evening.
3. **Inaccurate recaps**: Late minutes and early-leave minutes are computed against a single global window, producing meaningless figures for anyone not on the default schedule.
4. **Manual workaround**: Admins currently override every non-standard employee daily, which defeats the purpose of automated attendance.

## Goals

1. Define named **shift templates** with start time, end time, and whether the shift crosses midnight.
2. Assign shifts to employees — either a **fixed shift** or per-date overrides via a roster (including an explicit **day off**).
3. Compute lateness, early departure, and the absent sweep against each employee's **effective shift** on any given date, not the global setting.
4. Keep backward compatibility: employees without a shift assignment continue to use the global `staff_start_time` / `staff_end_time` (spec 16 default behavior).

## Non-goals

- Sub-daily / split shifts (e.g. 07:00–12:00 then 13:00–16:00). v1 models one continuous window per shift.
- Automatic shift rotation algorithms (e.g. 3-shift rotation generated by the system). Admins assign shifts manually or via roster.
- Roster bulk patterns beyond a plain date range (weekly repeat, CSV import). v1 ships range + shift only.
- Shift swap between employees (self-service). Deferred to v1.x; the admin edits the roster.
- Break time tracking within a shift.
- Overtime calculation or premium pay rates.
- Student-facing impact: shifts affect only employee attendance (spec 16). Student attendance (spec 03) is unchanged.

## User Stories

- **As an admin**, I want to create a "Shift Pagi" (07:00–14:00) and a "Shift Malam" (19:00–07:00) template, so I can assign them to different employee groups.
- **As an admin**, I want to set the security guard's default shift to "Shift Malam", so his 19:05 check-in is evaluated as 5 minutes late (not 12 hours late against the morning start).
- **As an admin**, I want to override one employee to "Shift Siang" next Wednesday, and to mark next Friday as a day off for a Mon–Fri employee, without touching their default assignment.
- **As a security guard on the night shift**, I want my monthly recap to show correct late minutes relative to 19:00, not 07:00.
- **As an admin**, I want the absent sweep to run at each shift's configured sweep time, so night-shift employees are not swept at 09:00 but at their own cutoff (e.g. 21:00).
- **As an admin**, I want employees without any shift assignment to keep working on the global hours, so I don't have to assign shifts to every teacher.

## Decisions

### Shift templates

- A **shift** is a named template: `name`, `start_time`, `end_time`, `crosses_midnight` (derived on save: true iff `end_time < start_time`; not user-editable), `sweep_offset_minutes` (minutes after `start_time` at which the absent sweep fires for this shift, default 120).
- `crosses_midnight`: when true, `end_time < start_time` (e.g. 19:00–07:00). A check-in at 19:05 on Monday belongs to the Monday night shift; a check-out at 06:50 on Tuesday closes that same record.
- Shifts are school-wide master data managed by the admin as a section of the Attendance Settings page.

### Shift assignment

Two layers, both optional:

1. **Default shift** (`employees.shift_id`): the employee's standard shift, used on all their days unless a roster row overrides it.
2. **Roster** (`shift_roster` table): per-employee, per-date override. Takes precedence over the default shift. A roster row with `shift_id = null` is an explicit **day off**: the employee is not swept that date, and taps that day follow the non-expected-day rule (normal record, extra day). Bulk assign = select employees + date range + shift (or day off); it upserts roster rows for every date in the range.

### Effective shift resolution

On any given date, an employee's effective shift is resolved as:

```
roster row for (employee_id, date)        // shift_id, or null = explicit day off
  ?? employee.shift (default)
    ?? global settings (staff_start_time / staff_end_time)
```

### Working days interaction

Roster rows change **which hours** apply on a date; they never change **which days** the employee is expected (spec 16 `EmployeeCalendar::isExpected`: school day + `working_days`). A roster row does not make a Saturday-scheduled employee sweepable on a non-operational day — taps on such days stay extra-day records. The one exception is the explicit day-off row, which *removes* sweep expectation for that date. An employee with `working_days = [1,2,3,4,5]` on a night shift is expected Mon–Fri evenings; their Friday record belongs to Friday even though the shift ends Saturday morning, and a Saturday-morning check-out on a non-school Saturday still closes it (the record follows the start date).

### Tap resolution amendments (spec 16)

- **Check-in**: `late_minutes` is computed against the effective shift's start time on the scan date (global `staff_start_time` when no shift applies). The record snapshots the effective `shift_id` at creation.
- **Closing an existing record** (check-out, absent-upgrade, debounce edge): late/early minutes are recomputed against the **record's snapshotted shift** — start on the record date, end on the record date plus one day when `crosses_midnight` — never against the effective shift of the tap date. Records with a null snapshot keep using the global times. Check-out tap overwrite is otherwise unchanged (latest tap wins, recomputes early leave).
- **Cross-midnight close**: if the employee has an open record from a previous date whose snapshotted shift crosses midnight, and the tap falls at or before that shift's end time (next calendar day), the tap closes that record instead of opening a new one for today.
- **Orphan early-morning tap**: a tap inside a cross-midnight window with **no** open previous record follows normal spec 16 logic — first tap of the day creates a check-in for the tap date (admin override corrects the rare miss).

### Absent sweep amendments

The current single sweep at `staff_absent_sweep_time` becomes **per-shift**:

- Each shift has its own sweep time, computed as `start_time + sweep_offset_minutes`.
- `routes/console.php` registers one run per distinct shift sweep time active today, following the existing settings-driven `dailyAt()` pattern (schedule definitions are re-evaluated every minute by `schedule:run`). The global `staff_absent_sweep_time` remains the sweep time for the no-shift fallback group.
- A run processes only employees whose effective shift that date matches (skipped: explicit day-off rows, non-working days, non-school days, inactive employees) and who have no record for that date. Idempotent via the `(employee_id, date)` unique constraint, as today.
- Implementation: `staff-attendance:mark-absences` gains `--shift={id}`; the no-flag invocation sweeps only employees with no effective shift that date. With zero shifts configured, behavior is identical to spec 16.

### Manual override amendment (spec 16 §Req 3)

`StaffAttendanceController::upsert` resolves the effective shift for the record date, snapshots it, and computes late/early minutes against it. For a cross-midnight shift, a check-out time earlier than the shift start parses as the **next** calendar day. Explicit-off dates can still be overridden manually (admin judgment wins).

### Missing check-out ("tidak absen pulang") amendment

A present/late record with no check-out counts as *tidak absen pulang* only once its **effective end** has passed: the snapshotted shift's end time (next day when cross-midnight), or the global `staff_end_time` for null snapshots. This corrects both the recap and the daily overview, which currently count any open past-date record — so a Monday 19:00–07:00 record still open at Tuesday 06:00 is mid-shift, not missing check-out, and a same-day open record is only counted after its end time.

### Notifications interaction (spec 17)

Night-shift absences swept at 21:00 or later fall inside the default quiet hours (21:00–06:00): the in-app notification is immediate, WhatsApp is deferred to morning. No change needed.

## Requirements

1. **Shift Template Management (Attendance Settings section, admin-only)**:
   - Managed as a "Shift Kerja" section on `/settings/attendance`; CRUD posts to flat resource routes (`settings/shifts`, `settings/shifts/{shift}`). No standalone page.
   - Fields: `name` (required, unique, max 100), `start_time` (required, HH:MM), `end_time` (required, HH:MM), `sweep_offset_minutes` (unsigned int, default 120, range 30–480). `crosses_midnight` is derived and displayed, not editable.
   - Validation: `start_time ≠ end_time`; `start_time + sweep_offset_minutes` must stay within the start date (a sweep time past 24:00 would fire the next day and mark that evening's not-yet-started shift absent).
   - Deletion blocked (HTTP 422, explanatory message) while any employee default, roster row, or attendance record references the shift.
   - Max 10 templates (UI simplicity; raise if needed).

2. **Employee Shift Assignment**:
   - `employees.shift_id` (nullable FK → `shifts.id`) editable on the employee form (admin).
   - Display the effective shift on the employee index and detail pages.

3. **Roster (`staff-attendance/roster`, flat URL)**:
   - Calendar-style grid: rows = employees, columns = dates (week or month view).
   - Each cell shows the effective shift (color-coded by shift; explicit day-off visually distinct) and can be overridden.
   - Bulk assign: select employees + date range + shift (or day off) → upsert roster rows for every date in the range.
   - Clear: remove a roster row → falls back to the default shift.
   - Only today and future dates are editable; past dates read-only.
   - Admin edits; principal views read-only (mirrors the staff attendance pages). Mutation attempts as principal → HTTP 403.
   - Entry path: reachable from the staff attendance daily overview page actions, per the navigation rule (no orphan surface).

4. **Effective Shift Display**:
   - Staff attendance daily overview (spec 16 §Req 3): show each employee's effective shift name and times alongside check-in/out and late/early metrics; the global times appear only as the fallback label for employees without a shift.
   - Monthly recap (spec 16 §Req 5): structure unchanged — late/early metrics are already stored per record.

5. **Tap Resolution with Shifts**:
   - `EmployeeScanService` resolves the effective shift for the scan date before creating a record, and snapshots `shift_id` on it.
   - Closing taps use the record's snapshotted shift as decided above, including the cross-midnight close rule.

6. **Per-Shift Absent Sweep**:
   - `staff-attendance:mark-absences` gains `--shift={id}` (optional) alongside the existing `--date`.
   - `routes/console.php` registers the per-shift runs from today's active shifts (defaults + roster) following the existing pattern.
   - The no-flag invocation sweeps the no-shift fallback group, preserving backward compatibility.

7. **Attendance Settings Page Amendment**:
   - `staff_start_time`, `staff_end_time`, `staff_absent_sweep_time` remain as the global fallback for employees without a shift.
   - Note on the page: these hours apply to employees without a shift assignment.

8. **Manual Override with Shifts**:
   - The daily-overview manual create/edit computes late/early minutes against the effective shift of the record date and snapshots it; cross-midnight checkout times parse as next-day per the decision above.

New UI strings ship through the shared Laravel lang files (spec 18); every new surface keeps a clickable entry path per role (workflow rule).

## Schema

### 1. `shifts` Table (New)

| Column | Type | Modifiers | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `id` | bigint | unsigned, primary key | |
| `name` | varchar(100) | not null, unique | Display name (e.g. "Shift Pagi", "Shift Malam") |
| `start_time` | time | not null | Shift start (e.g. 07:00, 19:00) |
| `end_time` | time | not null | Shift end (e.g. 14:00, 07:00) |
| `crosses_midnight` | boolean | not null, default: false | Derived on save: true when `end_time < start_time` |
| `sweep_offset_minutes` | smallint | unsigned, not null, default: 120 | Minutes after `start_time` to trigger the absent sweep |
| `created_at` / `updated_at` | timestamp | nullable | |

### 2. `shift_roster` Table (New)

| Column | Type | Modifiers | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `id` | bigint | unsigned, primary key | |
| `employee_id` | bigint | unsigned, not null | FK → `employees.id` |
| `date` | date | not null | Specific date for this override |
| `shift_id` | bigint | unsigned, nullable | FK → `shifts.id`; null = explicit day off |
| `created_at` / `updated_at` | timestamp | nullable | |

**Indexes:**
- `UNIQUE (employee_id, date)` — one roster row per employee per day
- `INDEX (date, shift_id)` — sweep query efficiency

### 3. Changes to existing tables

- `employees`: add `shift_id` (bigint, unsigned, nullable, FK → `shifts.id`) — the default shift.
- `employee_attendances`: add `shift_id` (bigint, unsigned, nullable, FK → `shifts.id`) — snapshot of the effective shift at record creation, for accurate closing taps and display even if assignments change later. The snapshot preserves the reference, not the historical times: editing a shift's times later changes what past records display, but stored `late_minutes` / `early_leave_minutes` never move.

No new settings keys.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-20-01**: An admin can create a "Shift Malam" (19:00–07:00) template; `crosses_midnight` is automatically set to true.
- **AC-20-02**: An employee assigned "Shift Malam" who checks in at 19:05 on Monday has `late_minutes = 5` and record `date = Monday`. If they check out at 06:50 Tuesday, `early_leave_minutes = 10` on the same Monday record.
- **AC-20-03**: The absent sweep at 21:00 (19:00 + 120min) marks absent only "Shift Malam" employees who have no record for today; it does not affect "Shift Pagi" employees or employees with no shift.
- **AC-20-04**: The 09:00 global sweep marks absent only employees without a shift assignment who have no record; it does not affect "Shift Malam" employees.
- **AC-20-05**: A roster row for employee A on Wednesday set to "Shift Siang" overrides their default "Shift Pagi"; Wednesday's lateness is computed against Shift Siang times.
- **AC-20-06**: Removing a roster row for a date falls back to the employee's default shift.
- **AC-20-07**: An employee with no shift and no roster row uses the global `staff_start_time`/`staff_end_time` — backward compatible with spec 16 behavior.
- **AC-20-08**: Deleting a shift template referenced by an employee default, a roster row, or an attendance record returns HTTP 422 with an explanatory message.
- **AC-20-09**: The daily staff attendance overview shows each employee's effective shift name alongside their check-in/out times and late/early metrics.
- **AC-20-10**: Cross-midnight: a tap at 06:50 Tuesday closes Monday's night-shift record (check-out), not a new check-in on Tuesday.
- **AC-20-11**: With the configured debounce window D: a check-in at 19:05 Monday followed by a tap within D minutes returns `ignored_debounce`; a tap at 19:05 Tuesday opens a new Tuesday record.
- **AC-20-12**: `employee_attendances.shift_id` snapshots the effective shift at record creation; changing the employee's default shift later does not retroactively alter past records' shift reference.
- **AC-20-13**: An explicit day-off roster row suppresses the absent sweep for that employee on that date (no `absent` record is created); a tap that day still creates a normal record, counted as an extra day in the recap.
- **AC-20-14**: A manual override for a shift employee computes late/early minutes against the effective shift of the record date; for a 19:00–07:00 shift, entering check-out 06:50 stores it as next-day 06:50 with `early_leave_minutes = 10`.
- **AC-20-15**: A Monday 19:00–07:00 record still open at Tuesday 06:00 is not counted as *tidak absen pulang* (recap and overview); once Tuesday 07:00 has passed with no check-out, it is.
- **AC-20-16**: A principal can view the roster but receives HTTP 403 on roster mutation and shift CRUD; a non-admin, non-principal user receives HTTP 403 on all three surfaces.

## Constraints & Assumptions

- Maximum of 10 shift templates in v1.
- Cross-midnight shifts cannot span more than 24 hours (`start_time ≠ end_time` forbids exactly 24h).
- Per-shift scheduling follows the existing settings-driven `dailyAt()` registration in `routes/console.php`; missed runs while the scheduler is down are not backfilled (same as the existing sweeps).
- Shift assignment changes affect only records created afterwards; existing records keep their snapshot (AC-20-12).
- Orphan early-morning taps (inside a cross-midnight window with no open record) become the tap date's check-in; correction is a manual override.
