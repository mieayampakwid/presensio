# 20 — Work Shifts (Jadwal Shift Kerja)

Status: draft v1.0 (2026-10-05) — resolves spec 16 non-goal "Shift scheduling" and open question "Per-employee working hours". Depends on spec 16.

> **Motivation:** Spec 16 assumes a single daily working window for all employees. In reality, non-teaching staff (security / satpam, cleaning / OB, lab assistants) frequently work on rotating shifts with different start/end times and may include evening or night shifts. Without shift support, these employees are incorrectly flagged as late or absent because their actual hours differ from the global `staff_start_time` / `staff_end_time`.

## Problem

1. **False lateness**: A night-shift security guard arriving at 19:00 is flagged as late because `staff_start_time` is 07:00.
2. **False absences**: The morning sweep at `staff_absent_sweep_time` (09:00) marks night-shift employees absent because they have no check-in before 09:00 — they are not expected until evening.
3. **Inaccurate recaps**: Late minutes and early-leave minutes are computed against a single global window, producing meaningless figures for anyone not on the default schedule.
4. **Manual workaround**: Admins currently override every non-standard employee daily, which defeats the purpose of automated attendance.

## Goals

1. Define named **shift templates** with start time, end time, and whether the shift crosses midnight.
2. Assign shifts to employees — either a **fixed shift** or a **rotating schedule** managed via a weekly roster.
3. Compute lateness, early departure, and the absent sweep against each employee's **effective shift** on any given date, not the global setting.
4. Keep backward compatibility: employees without a shift assignment continue to use the global `staff_start_time` / `staff_end_time` (spec 16 default behavior).

## Non-goals

- Sub-daily / split shifts (e.g. 07:00–12:00 then 13:00–16:00). v1 models one continuous window per shift.
- Automatic shift rotation algorithms (e.g. 3-shift rotation generated by the system). Admins assign shifts manually or via roster.
- Break time tracking within a shift.
- Overtime calculation or premium pay rates.
- Student-facing impact: shifts affect only employee attendance (spec 16). Student attendance (spec 03) is unchanged.

## User Stories

- **As an admin**, I want to create a "Shift Pagi" (07:00–14:00) and a "Shift Malam" (19:00–07:00) template, so I can assign them to different employee groups.
- **As an admin**, I want to set the security guard's default shift to "Shift Malam", so his 19:05 check-in is evaluated as 5 minutes late (not 12 hours late against the morning start).
- **As an admin**, I want to build a weekly roster for cleaning staff: Monday = Shift Pagi, Tuesday = Shift Siang, etc., so the system knows which shift applies on each day.
- **As a security guard on the night shift**, I want my monthly recap to show correct late minutes relative to 19:00, not 07:00.
- **As an admin**, I want the absent sweep to run at each shift's configured sweep time, so night-shift employees are not swept at 09:00 but at their own cutoff (e.g. 20:00).
- **As an admin**, I want employees without any shift assignment to keep working on the global hours, so I don't have to assign shifts to every teacher.

## Decisions

### Shift templates

- A **shift** is a named template: `name`, `start_time`, `end_time`, `crosses_midnight` (boolean), `sweep_offset_minutes` (minutes after `start_time` at which the absent sweep fires for this shift, default 120 = 2 hours).
- `crosses_midnight`: when true, `end_time < start_time` (e.g. 19:00–07:00). A check-in at 19:05 on Monday belongs to the Monday night shift; a check-out at 06:50 on Tuesday closes that same record.
- Shifts are school-wide resources managed by admin via settings UI.

### Shift assignment

Two modes, both optional:

1. **Default shift** (`employees.shift_id`): the employee's standard shift, used on all their working days unless overridden by a roster entry.
2. **Weekly roster** (`shift_roster` table): per-employee, per-date shift assignment. Takes precedence over the default shift. Admins can bulk-assign a roster pattern (e.g. repeat weekly for a month).

### Effective shift resolution

On any given date, an employee's effective shift is resolved as:

```
roster entry for (employee_id, date)
  ?? employee.shift (default)
    ?? global settings (staff_start_time / staff_end_time)
```

If no shift and no global settings, the employee is treated as unscheduled for that date (skip sweep, no late computation).

### Tap resolution amendments (spec 16)

Employee tap resolution (spec 16 §Decisions) is amended:

- **Late determination**: `checked_in_at > effective_start_time` → `late_minutes = diff`. For cross-midnight shifts, `effective_start_time` is on the calendar date.
- **Early departure**: `checked_out_at < effective_end_time` → `early_leave_minutes = diff`. For cross-midnight, `effective_end_time` is on the next calendar date.
- **Check-out tap overwrite**: unchanged (latest tap wins, recomputes early leave).

### Absent sweep amendments

The current single sweep at `staff_absent_sweep_time` becomes **per-shift**:

- Each shift has its own sweep time, computed as `start_time + sweep_offset_minutes`.
- The scheduler registers one sweep job per distinct shift `sweep_time` across active assignments for today. The global `staff_absent_sweep_time` becomes the sweep time for the "no shift" fallback group.
- A sweep run at time T processes only employees whose effective shift's sweep time ≤ T and who have no record for today.
- Implementation: the scheduled command accepts an optional `--shift={id}` flag. The scheduler queries distinct sweep times for today's active shifts and schedules a run for each. The existing `staff-attendance:mark-absences` (no flag) sweeps the fallback group.

### Cross-midnight record attribution

A cross-midnight shift's attendance record is attributed to the **start date**. Example:

- Shift "Malam": 19:00 Mon – 07:00 Tue.
- Check-in Mon 19:05 → record date = Monday, status = late (5 min).
- Check-out Tue 06:50 → same record, `checked_out_at` = Tue 06:50, `early_leave_minutes` = 10.
- If a second tap occurs Tue 19:05, that opens a **new** record for Tuesday's night shift.

Attribution window: a check-in is attributed to a cross-midnight shift if `checked_in_at` falls within `[start_time - 30min, end_time]` of the shift on that date. Taps outside this window follow the normal (non-cross-midnight) logic.

### Working days interaction

`employees.working_days` (spec 16) still determines **which days** the employee is expected. Shifts determine **which hours** on those days. An employee with `working_days = [1,2,3,4,5]` and a night shift is expected Mon–Fri evenings; they are not swept on Saturday even if their Friday night shift extends into Saturday morning (the record belongs to Friday).

## Requirements

1. **Shift Template CRUD (`/settings/shifts`)**:
   - Admin-only.
   - Fields: `name` (required, unique, max 100), `start_time` (required, HH:MM), `end_time` (required, HH:MM), `crosses_midnight` (boolean, auto-derived: true if `end_time < start_time`), `sweep_offset_minutes` (unsigned int, default 120, range 30–480).
   - Validation: `start_time ≠ end_time`.
   - Deletion blocked if any employee references the shift (default or roster).
   - Displayed on the Attendance Settings page as a sub-section.

2. **Employee Shift Assignment**:
   - `employees.shift_id` (nullable FK → `shifts.id`) editable on the employee form.
   - Display the effective shift on the employee index and detail pages.

3. **Weekly Roster (`/admin/staff-attendance/roster`)**:
   - Calendar-style grid: rows = employees, columns = dates (week or month view).
   - Each cell shows the effective shift (color-coded by shift) and can be overridden.
   - Bulk assign: select employees + date range + shift → insert/upsert roster entries.
   - Clear: remove roster entry → falls back to default shift.
   - Only future and today's dates are editable; past dates are read-only.

4. **Effective Shift Display**:
   - Staff attendance daily overview (spec 16 Req 3): show each employee's effective shift name and times alongside check-in/out and late/early metrics.
   - Monthly recap (spec 16 Req 5): no changes to the recap structure; late/early metrics are already per-record.

5. **Tap Resolution with Shifts**:
   - `EmployeeScanService` resolves the effective shift for the employee on the scan date before computing `late_minutes` and `early_leave_minutes`.
   - Cross-midnight: if the employee has an open (unclosed) record from yesterday's cross-midnight shift and taps now within `[yesterday_start, yesterday_end]`, close yesterday's record (check-out) instead of opening a new one.

6. **Per-Shift Absent Sweep**:
   - The scheduled command `staff-attendance:mark-absences` gains `--shift={id}` (optional).
   - The scheduler in `routes/console.php` dynamically registers runs based on today's active shift sweep times.
   - The no-flag invocation sweeps employees with no shift (global fallback), preserving backward compatibility.

7. **Settings Page Amendment**:
   - `staff_start_time`, `staff_end_time`, `staff_absent_sweep_time` remain as the global fallback for employees without a shift.
   - A note on the settings page: "These hours apply to employees without a specific shift assignment."

## Schema

### 1. `shifts` Table (New)

| Column | Type | Modifiers | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `id` | bigint | unsigned, primary key | |
| `name` | varchar(100) | not null, unique | Display name (e.g. "Shift Pagi", "Shift Malam") |
| `start_time` | time | not null | Shift start (e.g. 07:00, 19:00) |
| `end_time` | time | not null | Shift end (e.g. 14:00, 07:00) |
| `crosses_midnight` | boolean | not null, default: false | True when `end_time < start_time` |
| `sweep_offset_minutes` | smallint | unsigned, not null, default: 120 | Minutes after `start_time` to trigger the absent sweep |
| `created_at` / `updated_at` | timestamp | nullable | |

### 2. `shift_roster` Table (New)

| Column | Type | Modifiers | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `id` | bigint | unsigned, primary key | |
| `employee_id` | bigint | unsigned, not null | FK → `employees.id` |
| `date` | date | not null | Specific date for this override |
| `shift_id` | bigint | unsigned, not null | FK → `shifts.id` |
| `created_at` / `updated_at` | timestamp | nullable | |

**Indexes:**
- `UNIQUE (employee_id, date)` — one shift per employee per day
- `INDEX (date, shift_id)` — sweep query efficiency

### 3. Changes to existing tables

- `employees`: add `shift_id` (bigint, unsigned, nullable, FK → `shifts.id`) — the default shift.
- `employee_attendances`: add `shift_id` (bigint, unsigned, nullable, FK → `shifts.id`) — snapshot of the effective shift at scan time, for accurate recap even if assignments change later.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-20-01**: An admin can create a "Shift Malam" (19:00–07:00) template; `crosses_midnight` is automatically set to true.
- **AC-20-02**: An employee assigned "Shift Malam" who checks in at 19:05 on Monday has `late_minutes = 5` and record `date = Monday`. If they check out at 06:50 Tuesday, `early_leave_minutes = 10` on the same Monday record.
- **AC-20-03**: The absent sweep at 21:00 (19:00 + 120min) marks absent only "Shift Malam" employees who have no record for today; it does not affect "Shift Pagi" employees or employees with no shift.
- **AC-20-04**: The 09:00 global sweep marks absent only employees without a shift assignment who have no record; it does not affect "Shift Malam" employees.
- **AC-20-05**: A roster entry for employee A on Wednesday set to "Shift Siang" overrides their default "Shift Pagi"; Wednesday's lateness is computed against Shift Siang times.
- **AC-20-06**: Removing a roster entry for a date falls back to the employee's default shift.
- **AC-20-07**: An employee with no shift and no roster entry uses the global `staff_start_time`/`staff_end_time` — backward compatible with spec 16 behavior.
- **AC-20-08**: Deleting a shift template that is referenced by an employee or a roster entry returns HTTP 422 with an explanatory message.
- **AC-20-09**: The daily staff attendance overview shows each employee's effective shift name alongside their check-in/out times and late/early metrics.
- **AC-20-10**: Cross-midnight: a tap at 06:50 Tuesday is attributed as a check-out on Monday's night-shift record, not as a new check-in on Tuesday.
- **AC-20-11**: A cross-midnight shift employee who taps at 19:05 Monday (check-in, Monday) and again at 19:10 Monday (within debounce) gets `ignored_debounce`; a tap at 19:05 Tuesday opens a new Tuesday record.
- **AC-20-12**: `employee_attendances.shift_id` snapshots the effective shift at scan time; changing the employee's default shift later does not retroactively alter past records' shift reference.

## Constraints & Assumptions

- Maximum of 10 shift templates in v1 (UI simplicity; raise if needed).
- Cross-midnight shifts cannot span more than 24 hours.
- The scheduler must support dynamic scheduling (Laravel's `Schedule::call()` or conditional `when()`); Octane compatibility requires stateless schedule definition.
- Shift changes take effect from the next working day; mid-shift reassignment is not supported.
